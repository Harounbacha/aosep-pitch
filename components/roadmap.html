Roadmap

From prototype
to national platform

A phased, ambitious plan to scale AOSEP across Algeria and eventually across the MENA region.

Phase 1
0 – 6 months
Foundation & Beta Launch
  • Complete backend API & production database
  • User authentication with role-based access
  • Mentorship matching algorithm
  • Initial content library (Web Dev, Data Science, Marketing)
  • Closed beta: 50–100 users in Batna & Algiers
  • Global Opportunities Hub beta testing
Phase 2
6 – 18 months
Growth & Partnerships
  • Partner with 3+ Algerian universities
  • Onboard 10+ industry partners
  • 5+ international scholarship providers
  • First physical training center pilot
  • Expand to 10 learning tracks
  • 1,000 registered learners
Phase 3
18 – 36 months
National Scale & Beyond
  • AI-powered recommendation engine
  • Training centers in 5+ wilayas
  • Government partnership for credential recognition
  • 100,000 active learners nationally
  • Direct scholarship pipelines with overseas universities
  • Explore MENA regional expansion
