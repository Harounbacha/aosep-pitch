The Problem

Six barriers Algerian
learners face daily

Despite growing digital access, the path from learning to meaningful employment remains fragmented, isolated, and disconnected from global opportunity.

01
🗺️
No Structured Learning Paths
Platforms offer isolated courses without coherent progression frameworks tailored to local career contexts.
02
🔨
Limited Practical Experience
Academic programs emphasize theoretical knowledge at the expense of hands-on, project-based learning.
03
🚧
Hard to Access Local Jobs
Learners who build skills still encounter systemic barriers when seeking internships and entry-level roles.
04
🌍
No Global Opportunity Access
No centralized channel to discover international scholarships, exchanges, remote work, or overseas education.
05
✈️
Zero Study Abroad Guidance
No platform offers end-to-end guidance on international applications, visa processes, or scholarship eligibility.
06
🏭
Weak Industry Connection
Formal institutions operate in isolation from industry, creating a chronic skills mismatch for graduates.
The Solution

Three pillars.
One ecosystem.

AOSEP integrates structured learning, community training, and global opportunity access into a single, cohesive platform designed for the Algerian context.

PILLAR / 01
📚
Structured Learning Paths
Competency-based tracks with milestones, projects, and skill checkpoints — tailored to Algerian career contexts and global market demands.
  • Directed graph of learning modules
  • Prerequisite tracking & branching
  • Portfolio-building projects
  • Skill badge certification
  • Arabic, French & English support
PILLAR / 02
🤝
Peer-Led Training
Community mentorship, hackathons, and peer-review learning sessions — bringing the ecosystem into communities with or without reliable internet.
  • Physical training center model
  • Peer instructor tools & recognition
  • Hackathons & community events
  • Cohort-based collaborative learning
  • Reputation & endorsement system
PILLAR / 03
🌐
Global Opportunities Hub
Scholarships, exchanges, remote work, study-abroad guidance, and international programs — all in one searchable, filtered, actionable hub.
  • International scholarship database
  • Exchange programs directory
  • Remote work & freelance listings
  • Step-by-step overseas guidance
  • International mentors network

Roadmap

From prototype
to national platform

A phased, ambitious plan to scale AOSEP across Algeria and eventually across the MENA region.

Phase 1
0 – 6 months
Foundation & Beta Launch
  • Complete backend API & production database
  • User authentication with role-based access
  • Mentorship matching algorithm
  • Initial content library (Web Dev, Data Science, Marketing)
  • Closed beta: 50–100 users in Batna & Algiers
  • Global Opportunities Hub beta testing
Phase 2
6 – 18 months
Growth & Partnerships
  • Partner with 3+ Algerian universities
  • Onboard 10+ industry partners
  • 5+ international scholarship providers
  • First physical training center pilot
  • Expand to 10 learning tracks
  • 1,000 registered learners
Phase 3
18 – 36 months
National Scale & Beyond
  • AI-powered recommendation engine
  • Training centers in 5+ wilayas
  • Government partnership for credential recognition
  • 100,000 active learners nationally
  • Direct scholarship pipelines with overseas universities
  • Explore MENA regional expansion
